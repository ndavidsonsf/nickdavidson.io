I design the machinery underneath customer success - segmentation, health scoring, success planning, the renewal calendar - and then I stay in the room while it earns its keep with buyers who answer to a regulator.

Most CS teams are staffed before they are designed. The playbook arrives after the first churn, the health score is built for a board slide rather than for the CSM at 9am on a Tuesday, and expansion is a heroic act instead of a repeatable one.

I do the unglamorous version: fewer dashboards, tighter definitions, a handoff ritual from pre-sales that actually holds. Twelve years of it, most recently across a $25M enterprise portfolio in financial services.
